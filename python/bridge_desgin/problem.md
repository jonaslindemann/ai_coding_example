A pedestrian bridge is to cross a 20 m wide stream. The deck is 2.5 m wide and is carried by two identical steel trusses, one on each side. Design for a crowd load of 4 kN/m² on the deck and ignore self-weight in this first version. The steel is S355. The midspan deflection under crowd load must not exceed L/400. No member may yield, and no compression member may buckle. Which truss type (Pratt, Howe or Warren), height and member size gives the lightest design?
